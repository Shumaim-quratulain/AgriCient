Add a coconut land

A pin marks one point on this land; it does not map the boundary.
Visit each plot and capture its pin separately.
Land pins are saved to your account on the server and are visible only when you are signed in. GPS access requires your permission and a secure connection (HTTPS or localhost). Only a pin is saved—not your live location or the land boundary.

Weather risk at your lands

Based on the last 7 days of weather at your home location and at each saved land’s pin.

This is a weather-based risk score (humidity, rain and temperature), not a measured disease outbreak. The 3 day number drives your alerts; the 7 and 14 day numbers are only a rough outlook and are much less certain. The score was built using weather from three coconut areas (Tiptur, Kozhikode, Pollachi), so for other places treat it as a guide and confirm with an agricultural officer.

Area risk dashboard

Weather-based coconut disease risk for each area (about 1 km) where farmers are registered.

Areas are shown at about 1 km, never at an individual farmer’s land, and no farmer names or contact details are shown. The score is a weather-based risk index, not a count of confirmed disease cases. The map loads its tiles from OpenStreetMap and its code from unpkg.com.
